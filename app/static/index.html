Current SAIL Status Quo

Reactive Daily Spot Booking
  • Market Price Taker: Enters spot market on parcel arrival day regardless of peak rates.
  • Manual Draft Verification: High risk of draft overruns or unexpected demurrage at restricted berths.
  • Zero Forward Timing Intelligence: Cannot anticipate rate dips during Chinese New Year or Asian monsoon slowdowns.

Intelligent Anticipatory Engine

Data-Driven 'Wait vs. Book' Optimization
  • Multi-Horizon Rate Forecasting: Predicts BCI/BPI sub-indices with 80% & 95% confidence bands.
  • Deterministic Port Gatekeeper: Verifies LOA, beam, and dynamic load draft for all 7 East Coast ports.
  • Net Cost Optimization: Computes rate savings minus daily holding cost to pinpoint the exact booking day.
